Privacy policy

Pruva privacy policy · version 1 · 5 October 2026

Pruva processes boat measurements on the device. There is no user account, server, advertising SDK or automatic analytics upload.

Location and boat data

Race tracks you explicitly start store phone or NMEA GPS positions in a local archive. With your permission, phone GPS recording can continue while the screen is locked. The boat connection receives GPS, heading, speed and wind sentences over local Wi-Fi. Raw NMEA and decision snapshots are stored only when you start Field recording. Connection profiles store gateway addresses on the device.

Voice and language assistance

The microphone turns on only when you start listening. Audio files are not archived. If on-device speech recognition is unavailable for the selected language, use typed commands; recognition does not fall back to a server. Optional language assistance uses Apple's on-device model; RaceEngine produces numerical decisions.

Watch, lock screen and sharing

When enabled, the Watch companion sends summary data to the paired watch. Live Activities can show start/layline summaries on the lock screen, subject to system privacy settings. Stories, GPX, CSV, decision notes, field recordings and backups are sent to an app you choose only through your sharing action. GPX/CSV and backups contain real positions; check the recipient before sharing.

Retention and deletion

Records remain on the device. Normal deletion removes the selected race or decision from the main archive. Original file copies made during recovery are retained separately so the operation can be undone; uninstalling the app deletes its entire container. Delete races and decisions in Logbook, profiles in Boat, and raw sessions in Field recording. Delete exported backups, recovery copies and shared files separately from your own storage. Depending on iOS settings, device backups may include the data in your Apple device backup. Pruva does not send an additional copy to a server. Revoke location, microphone, speech and local network permissions in iOS Settings.

File access

Pruva reads file metadata and timestamps within its own container for listing and maintenance. During import, it checks metadata and size only for the file you select, to enforce safe reading limits. It does not fingerprint devices or users.

Questions

Support and issue reporting in the app can prepare a report draft without your data. A published support address appears there when configured.

Get in touch

Email your question, app version and steps to reproduce. Include locations, race tracks or raw instrument data only if you choose to share them.
